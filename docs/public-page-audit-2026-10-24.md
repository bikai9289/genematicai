## Evidence for SEO corrections

- [Google: sitemap lastmod and ignored changefreq/priority](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/06/sitemaps-lastmod-ping): lastmod is useful when accurate; omitting uncertain dates is permitted.
- [Google: FAQ rich-result restrictions](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes): regular FAQ rich results are restricted to well-known authoritative government and health sites.
- [Google: SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide): no magical minimum word count; meta keywords are unused. A mismatch deserves correction, but the claimed automatic URL-number penalty is not established by these documents.
- [Google: retirement of sitelinks search box](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2024/10/sitelinks-search-box): removed starting November 21, 2024; unused markup itself does not affect ranking.
